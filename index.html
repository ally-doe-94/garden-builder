      Scanning your garden

      1. Get a scanning app on your phone: Polycam, Scaniverse, Luma AI, KIRI Engine or RealityScan all work. iPhone Pro / iPad Pro models with LiDAR give the quickest results.
      2. Pick a bright, overcast day. Harsh sun and moving shadows make scans patchy.
      3. Walk slowly around the edge of the garden with the phone at chest height, pointing slightly down. Then do a second loop pointing more steeply at the ground. Overlap is good.
      4. Export as GLB (best: textured mesh), or PLY (point cloud or Gaussian splat) or OBJ.
      5. Load it here with Load scan or drag the file onto the page. Use Scan settings to fix orientation or scale if needed.

      Using the planner

      • Pick a plant on the left, then click/tap in the garden to plant it. Keep clicking to plant more; press Esc to stop.
      • Click a plant to select it: move, rotate, resize, add notes.
      • Open the Landscaping tab to draw paths, paving, garden beds, retaining walls, fences and steps. Click points in the garden, then double-click or press Enter to finish. Hold Shift for neat angles. Click a feature to change its material or size, or drag its white dots to reshape it. It also tells you roughly how much material you'll need.
      • Slide Growth to see how big things get over the years, and Month/Time to check sun, shade and the seasons.
      • Everything is saved in this browser automatically. Use ⋯ → Save layout file to back up or move to another device.
